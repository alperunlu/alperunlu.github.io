Privacy Policy

Sol de Cuba · last updated 2 September 2026

Sol de Cuba collects nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services. It makes no network requests at all. Nothing about you or your device leaves your phone.

What the app stores

The game keeps your progress — which chapter you have unlocked, whether the campaign is finished, your best Endless streak and whether you muted the sound — in local storage on the device itself. This never leaves the phone, is not backed up to us, and is deleted when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information of any kind.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not contain third-party SDKs that gather data.
  • It does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
  • It does not connect to the internet, so no data can be transmitted.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, children included, because it does not collect data at all.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the App Store contact form.

Sol de Cuba is an offline single-player game. This policy covers the iOS application distributed on the App Store.
