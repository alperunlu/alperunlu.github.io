Privacy Policy

Your kitchen, your data

Last updated: 7 August 2026

chef+ does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or device identifiers. Specifically, the app:

  • Does not require an account, sign-in, or registration
  • Does not connect to the internet or any remote server
  • Does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • Does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos
  • Contains no in-app purchases
  • Stores no data outside your device

Everything you configure — household size, dietary preferences, your meal plans and shopping lists — is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is ever uploaded, synced, or readable by us. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Children's privacy

chef+ is safe for all ages. Because the app collects no data and makes no network connections, it holds no personal information about children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

chef+ has no way to receive or transmit information, so this policy cannot change without an app update. Any future update is covered by the wording above; a revised policy would accompany that update.
