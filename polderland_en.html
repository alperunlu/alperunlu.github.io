Polderland

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Nederlands

Polderland does not collect any data.

What the app does not do

  • It makes no network requests. The whole game is contained in the app, and it runs the same way with the device in aeroplane mode.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting code, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • It does not ask for, receive or store your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or device identifiers.
  • It does not create an account and has nothing to log in to.

What is stored on your device

Your settings, your progress through the scenarios, your best scores and a save of the scenario you are part-way through are stored inside the app's own storage on your device. This never leaves the device, is not readable by other apps, and is deleted when you delete Polderland.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for children. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If a future version of Polderland ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support address listed on the App Store page for Polderland.
