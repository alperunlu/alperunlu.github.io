Privacy Policy for ColorPicker

Last updated: September 3, 2026

ColorPicker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or device identifiers. Specifically, the app:

  • Does not require an account, sign-in, or registration
  • Does not connect to the internet or any remote server
  • Does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • Does not access contacts, location, microphone, or your photo library
  • Contains no in-app purchases
  • Stores no data outside your device

Camera

ColorPicker uses your device's camera to sample the color of whatever the crosshair is pointed at. Camera frames are processed entirely on your device — nothing captured by the camera is ever saved to disk, uploaded, or sent anywhere. Each captured frame is used only to compute a color value and is then discarded.

Clipboard

When you tap a color value (HEX, RGB, HSL, or CMYK), the app copies that text to your device's clipboard using the operating system's standard clipboard API. This is a local action; the app has no way to read your clipboard's other contents and nothing is transmitted off your device.

Children's privacy

ColorPicker does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
